Top 3 Spotify Artists


Oasis

Oasis is an English rock band that consisted of Liam Gallagher, Noel Gallagher, Paul Arthurs, Paul McGuigan, and Tony McCarroll.





Green Day

Green Day is an American punk and rock band that consists of Billie Joe Armstrong as the lead singer and guitarist, Mike Dirnt as their bassist, and Tre Cool as their drummer.





Eraserheads

Eraserheads is a Filipino rock band formed in 1987. They held the title of "The Beatles of the Philippines," due to their impact on OPM, or Original Pilipino Music. The band consists of lead singer, Ely Buendia, guitarist Marcus Adoro, bassist Buddy Zabala, and drummer Raimund Marasigan.
